About This Analysis

This report is a portfolio demonstration piece prepared by Rahmananda Ridho Gamawan. It is based on the "Coffee Sales" dataset published on Kaggle by Ahmed Abbas, representing transaction records for Maven Roasters — a fictitious coffee shop chain operating across three New York City locations. The business, brand, customer profiles, and operating context described here are entirely fictional. All findings, recommendations, and uplift estimates are derived from the sample data and stated analytical assumptions. Uplift figures are modeled projections, not measured outcomes. They should not be interpreted as actual business performance, validated operational guidance, or investment advice.

The Right Product
at the Right Time.

Each store has a different opportunity window. The strategy below is tailored to each store's customer mission and demand rhythm — not a uniform network playbook.

Lower Manhattan
Protect and Monetize the Morning Rush
"The window is 6–10 AM. That's the entire game."
⏰ Target Daypart: Morning Rush (6–10 AM · 54% of daily revenue)
Push Products
  • Sustainably Grown Organic Lg
  • Latte Rg
  • Dark Chocolate Lg
  • Cappuccino Lg
Attach / Bundle
  • Chocolate Croissant
  • Ginger Scone
Grab-and-go items only. Pre-bag and place at eye level by 6 AM.
How to Execute
  • Put a small sign at the counter showing the coffee + croissant pairing and its price
  • Train staff to ask one question when handing over the drink: "Would you like a scone with that?"
  • Make sure all bakery items are fully stocked and visible on the counter by 6:00 AM
  • Check bakery stock and sales numbers at the end of the first 90 minutes every morning
KPIs to track: Bakery attach rate during 6–10 AM (target: +3pp from baseline) · Avg transaction value morning window (target: +$0.50/transaction) · Morning rush revenue as % of daily (protect at 54%+)

How the Uplift Was Estimated
1
Target daypart transaction volume. For each store, the number of daily transactions in the target daypart was estimated using total line items ÷ operating days × daypart %, giving the baseline exposure window.
2
Current attach rate baseline. The current secondary category (bakery) attach rate during the target daypart was estimated from actual line-item data at each store location.
3
+3pp conservative incremental improvement. A +3 percentage point increase in attach rate was applied — conservative relative to the 3–8pp range typically seen in retail upsell programs. This was multiplied by avg bakery item price and annualized to 180 operating days.
These estimates are directional order-of-magnitude figures only. Actual results depend on execution quality, staff training, signage effectiveness, and customer mix. Results should be treated as a sizing benchmark, not a forecast. Recommend setting a 90-day baseline measurement period before evaluating program performance.
Appendix

Data & Methodology Notes

For technical reviewers and portfolio audiences who want to understand how this analysis was built.

Dataset

Source: "Coffee Sales" dataset published on Kaggle by Ahmed Abbas. Dataset: Maven Roasters — a fictional NYC coffee chain. Rows: 149,116 transaction line items. Columns: transaction_id, transaction_date, transaction_time, transaction_qty, store_id, store_location, product_id, unit_price, product_category, product_type, product_detail. Period: January 1 – June 30, 2023. Each row represents a single product line item from a single visit.

Data Structure

The dataset is structured as one product per row. Transaction IDs appear to be unique per line item, meaning direct Apriori basket analysis at the product level is not feasible. Category-level analysis was conducted instead, treating store × date × hour blocks as the unit of association. All revenue figures represent estimated gross sales (unit_price × transaction_qty).

Analytical Methods

EDA conducted in Python (pandas). Revenue share, category mix, and daypart breakdowns derived from groupby aggregations on the full dataset. Daypart buckets: Morning Rush = before 11 AM, Midday = 11 AM–2 PM, Late Day = after 2 PM. Hourly charts reflect aggregate revenue by hour across the full 6-month period. Category over/under-index computed as store % ÷ network % for each category.

Limitations & Caveats

No individual receipt-level basket data available — bundle recommendations are based on category co-occurrence patterns and top-performing products by store, not measured cross-sell rates. Uplift estimates use a uniform +3pp attach improvement assumption across all stores — actual improvement will vary. All customer persona descriptions are inferred from demand pattern data, not from customer survey or loyalty data. This is a fictional dataset; all conclusions apply only within this analytical frame.
